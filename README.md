1. Project Overview
- This project contains a data set from an imaginary chain of restaurant called Zomato. The chain owner wants to improve his restaurants' ratings, but from the current data set, it's unclear how these variables change between markets and how they will affect the ratings.
- The goal is to provide basic EDA of the data set, in order to explore restaurant trends, customer preferences, pricing patterns, cuisine popularity and factors associated with restaurant ratings. From the basic analysis, we can identify patterns of successful restaurant and give a good basis for further analysis.

2. Analytical Questions
- What are the price ranges?
- What is the most common cuisine?
- How are the restaurants distributed?
- How are ratings determined?
- Are ratings associated with prices?
- Does more services availability give higher ratings?
- Does votes affect ratings?

3. Initial Hypothesis
- Higher price range will lead to higher ratings
- Restaurants that has online delivery tend to have higher ratings
- A small number of cuisine are implemented in the majority of the restaurants
- Restaurants with higher votes tend to have different ratings
- The data set contains some duplicate records, missing values, or inconsistent formating

4. Data Dictionary
- Restaurant ID         Unique ID of restaurants in the chain  
- Restaurant Name       Name of the restaurants
- Country Code          Country code of the restaurants
- City                  City that the restaurant is in
- Address               The address of the restaurant
- Locality              The relative area of the city that the restaurant is in
- Locality Verbose      The verbose version of Locality
- Longitude             The longtitude of the location of the restaurant
- Latitude              The latitude of the location of the restaurant
- Cuisines              Type of cuisines of the restaurant 
- Average Cost for two  Average cost for a two-people meal
- Currency              The currency of the locality
- Has Table booking     Availability of table booking service
- Has Online delivery   Availability of online delivery service
- Is delivering now     Availability of delivery at the momnet
- Switch to order menu  Bool
- Price range           Int from 1 to 4
- Aggregate rating      The average rating of the restaurant
- Rating color          The color indicating the rating of the restaurant
- Rating text           The description fo the color rating of the restaurant
- Votes                 Total votes of the restaurant
